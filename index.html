My Plants

Care cards and a simple watering tracker for everything growing at home. Tap “Watered today” and the card keeps count for you.

Golden rule: these intervals are a starting point. Push a finger 2–3 cm into the soil — if it’s still damp, wait a few more days. Always water thoroughly and let the excess drain out.

Growing season = roughly April–September, resting season = October–March (the page picks automatically, you can switch it). Watering history is saved only in this browser.
